## Method

**Identification.** Risks are listed per asset (see `asset-inventory-and-classification.md`) as a threat exploiting a vulnerability with a consequence for confidentiality, integrity or availability. Sources: technical review findings (ids P-, F-, S-, D-, AU-, A- in the findings set of 2026-10), provider changes, incidents, and the annual review.

**Likelihood (L)**: 1 = unlikely in 3 years; 2 = plausible within a year; 3 = expected within a year or already observed.

**Impact (I)**: 1 = minor (no personal data, recoverable in hours, no notification); 2 = significant (some users' personal data or service down > 1 day, or a public promise broken); 3 = severe (all users' email/data exposed, legal notification required, or permanent loss).

**Score** = L × I (1–9).

**Acceptance threshold**: score ≤ 2 may be accepted by the owner without treatment. Score 3–4 requires a treatment with a planned date. Score ≥ 6 requires treatment to start within 30 days and is reported at management review until closed.

**Treatment options**: modify (add a control), accept (documented, owner signs), avoid (remove the activity), share (contract/provider/insurance). Residual risk after treatment is re-scored and must be ≤ 4 unless explicitly accepted.

**Risk owner** is always the owner (Mark) unless delegated to the Spencer role for engineering treatments; the register names which.

**Review**: annually, after every incident severity 1–2, and on any change of provider, scope or sign-in method.
