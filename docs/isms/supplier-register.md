## Supplier controls

- **Onboarding** a new provider: add a row here, update `scope.md`, the risk register, the SoA, the privacy page, and obtain the DPA before production use.
- **Monitoring** (A.5.22): subscribe to status pages (status.supabase.com, netlifystatus.com, githubstatus.com, resend-status.com) and to each provider's security notification email; review this register annually or on any provider incident, pricing change or terms change.
- **Change**: a provider changing region, sub-processors or terms triggers a privacy-page review and, for region, a risk register update (R-14).
- **Cloud services** (A.5.23): configuration of each service is in the repository (`netlify.toml`, `supabase/config.toml`) or documented in `docs/`; dashboard-only settings are listed in `docs/SUPABASE.md` and checked in the quarterly access review.
